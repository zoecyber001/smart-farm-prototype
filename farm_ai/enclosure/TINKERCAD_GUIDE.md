# Smart Farm Field Node: 3D Enclosure (Concept Model) Guide

> **Status: CONCEPT PROTOTYPE.** The STLs below are a parametric box-primitive model
> for visualising and demoing the node's packaging. They are **not** weatherproof:
> there are no drilled screw holes, no cable-gland / antenna / vent cutouts printed
> in them, and **no ingress rating (IP65 or otherwise) has been tested or claimed**.
> For outdoor use, treat this as the starting shell and add the cutouts from the
> checklist in Section 5.
> **Tools that open these files:** TinkerCad (Import), Fusion 360, FreeCAD, Blender.
> Recommended print material for the real build: PETG / ASA / ABS (UV-safe).
> **Included 3D STL Files:** [`enclosure_base.stl`](enclosure_base.stl) & [`enclosure_lid.stl`](enclosure_lid.stl)
> Generated by [`generate_enclosure.py`](generate_enclosure.py); verified by [`validate_stl.py`](validate_stl.py).

---

## 1. Actual geometry the STL files contain

The generator builds plain axis-aligned box primitives and unions them in the
slicer. That keeps the files dead simple to import, but it also means:
sharp corners (no `Radius` fillets), solid bosses (no pilot holes), and solid
walls (no cutouts). Here is exactly what is inside each file:

### `enclosure_base.stl` — 130 × 90 × 45 mm (13 boxes, 156 facets)

| Feature | Position / size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Floor slab | X 0–130, Y 0–90, Z 0–3 | 3 mm base |
| Left / right walls | X 0–3 and 127–130, Y 0–90, Z 3–45 | 3 mm vertical walls |
| Front / back walls | X 3–127, Y 0–3 and 87–90, Z 3–45 | 3 mm vertical walls |
| 4 corner mounting bosses | 10 × 10 × 42 mm, inner corners (e.g. X 3–13, Y 3–13) | **Solid** — drill your own M3 pilot (Ø2.8) if using screws |
| 4 PCB standoffs | 6 × 6 × 6 mm, at (25,25), (75,25), (25,60), (75,60), Z 3–9 | For the ESP32 carrier / relay board |
| Model volume | 106,692 mm³ | Checked by `validate_stl.py` |

### `enclosure_lid.stl` — 130 × 90 mm flat lid (8 boxes, 96 facets)

| Feature | Position / size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lid slab (4 segments around the window) | Z 0–5 | 5 mm thick |
| OLED window (hole through the lid) | X 35–61, Y 38–52 (26 × 14 mm) | Matches the 0.96" OLED's active area; glue an acrylic sheet behind it to seal |
| Perimeter tongue ("lip") | 1.8 × 2.0 mm, hangs **down** 2 mm from the lid underside, inset 3.5 mm from each edge | Sits inside the base cavity when the lid is placed on the rim (a press-fit tongue, **not** a gasket seal) |
| Lid bbox height | Z −2 … +5 mm | The −2 mm is the downward tongue |
| Model volume | 58,163.2 mm³ (facet-orientation parity) | Checked by `validate_stl.py` |

`validate_stl.py` confirms per file: record/table integrity, finite coordinates,
every facet's orientation consistent with its stored normal, exact bounding box,
facet count and signed volume. Run `python3 validate_stl.py` after any edit to
`generate_enclosure.py`.

> **Known gap vs the original design sketch:** the team's presentation-screenshot
> assembly from TinkerCad shows extra plumbing (glands, antenna, flanges) that is
> **not** present in these committed STLs, and the screenshot itself is not stored
> in this repository, so the two cannot be diffed here. The STLs are the source of
> truth for what will come out of a printer today; use Section 4 to model the rest.

## 2. Option A: Quick Import into TinkerCad (~15 seconds)

1. Log into [tinkercad.com](https://www.tinkercad.com) and create a new 3D Design.
2. In the top-right toolbar, click **Import**.
3. Choose `farm_ai/enclosure/enclosure_base.stl` and click **Import**.
   * The base with corner bosses and PCB standoffs appears on the workplane.
4. Click **Import** again and select `farm_ai/enclosure/enclosure_lid.stl`.
   * The lid with the viewing window and downward tongue appears next to it.
5. Position the lid straight above the base rim; the tongue then drops into the cavity.

## 3. Option B: Manual enhancements in TinkerCad (make it setup-ready)

Import the STL, or model the base from scratch (solid 130×90×45 box, hollowed by a
124×84×45 hole-box raised 3 mm), then add:

1. **Cable entry cutouts (bottom wall, entries face down so water never pools):**
   drag a hole cylinder Ø12.5 mm (PG7 gland clearance), rotate 90° to point vertically,
   and punch three holes through the floor at X = 30, 65, 100 mm — one each for the
   soil probe, flow sensor and 12 V pump cable.
2. **Antenna cutout (right wall):** hole cylinder Ø6.5 mm at (X = 127, Y = 70, Z = 32).
   *Only needed with a real LoRa module with a bulkhead SMA — the Wokwi demo has none.*
3. **Vent plug hole (left wall):** Ø12.0 mm for an M12 ePTFE breather at mid-height.
4. **Lid / boss screw holes:** hole cylinders Ø3.5 mm through the bosses' tops or
   Ø2.8 mm self-tap pilots; add matching holes in the lid 8 mm in from each corner.
5. Optional cosmetics: shape fillets, post-mount flanges, a label emboss on the lid.

Group each hole with the STL body, then re-export.

## 5. Weatherproofing checklist (design intent for the real build)

These are the requirements for an outdoor-ready enclosure — the concept STLs
implement none of them; they are what you should verify before field beta:

1. **Gasketed mating surface:** the printed tongue is a press-fit only. A real
   seal needs an EPDM/gasket or silicone bead between lid and base, or the tongue
   sits inside a channel — pick one and test with a hose spray, not an IP number.
2. **Downward cable glands:** all cables enter through the floor via compression
   glands matched to each cable's OD.
3. **Drip loops:** every cable loops down before entering its gland.
4. **Sealed viewing window:** acrylic or polycarbonate sheet glued behind the lid
   window with neutral-cure silicone; OLED faces the window.
5. **Condensation control:** 5 g silica desiccant pack inside + M12 ePTFE vent plug.
6. **Mounting:** on a post with the floor **at least 40 cm above soil level**, clear
   of splash, shade from direct sun. Optional U-bolt through modified flank ears.

**Do not quote "IP65" about these files** — that rating belongs only to whatever
final assembly you actually test.

## 6. Honest limitations for the presentation

* Both STLs are **concept shells**: correct proportions, correct window, correct
  downward tongue, and verified facet integrity — but unfinished for fabrication.
* Hardware behaviours referenced elsewhere (gland sealing, weather, LCD readability
  in sunlight) are **design intent**, not demonstrated properties.
* The Tinkercad screenshot used in class materials is not stored in the repo and
  shows an enlarged/embellished assembly; regenerate it from the committed STLs
  for screenshots so what you show equals what you have.
